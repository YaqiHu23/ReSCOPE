# Third-party preprocessing and downstream components

- **UNI2-h:** [Mahmood Lab model and access conditions](https://huggingface.co/MahmoodLab/UNI2-h), [source project](https://github.com/mahmoodlab/UNI). The wrapper uses the 1536-dimensional UNI2-h architecture through timm. Obtain weights through your own approved Hugging Face account. Weights are not redistributed; their terms apply separately from this repository's MIT license.
- **CellViT:** [official repository](https://github.com/TIO-IKIM/CellViT), pinned commit `05097e18e3d194a65121042f631b5753069f5ee3`. Setup downloads the repository separately. Its Apache 2.0 with Commons Clause license and checkpoint terms remain with that checkout. See the upstream README for checkpoint downloads and publication attribution.
- **BLEEP:** [official repository](https://github.com/bowang-lab/BLEEP), pinned commit `23959670c7407510c1716a173184bdcc18833a35`, Apache-2.0. Downloaded separately by setup; no upstream source or weights are vendored here. Cite the BLEEP NeurIPS 2023 paper linked by its README. Our adapters retain ResNet50, reference-cell SVD, contrastive training and weighted-neighbor expression prediction from the project experiments.
- **iSTAR:** [official repository](https://github.com/daviddaiweizhang/istar), pinned commit `3cb0e5352a86df337f41c5841d0808da0003457b`, GPL-3.0. Downloaded separately by setup. Cite Zhang et al., *Nature Biotechnology* (2024), DOI: [10.1038/s41587-023-02019-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-023-02019-9), and the upstream HIPT/ViT acknowledgements. `patches/istar-training.patch` is a modification of upstream `impute.py` and `train.py`, distributed under GPL-3.0 (see [license text](licenses/iSTAR-GPL-3.0.txt)); it is **not** covered by this repository's MIT license. It records training loss, exposes the learning rate, and retains the lowest-training-loss checkpoint, matching the existing paper workflow. The upstream copyright notices remain with the downloaded source.

The downstream adapters derive from this project's ROI splitting, BLEEP and iSTAR experiment runners. Paths/hashes are recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`. Only the selected 1/2/3-ROI workflow is packaged. The public adapters use explicit full-resolution x,y coordinates and a user-supplied fixed gene panel. Environment versions, fresh seeds and public pretrained weights do not imply bitwise reproduction of private historical checkpoints. No tissue-trained models or tissue data are redistributed. The optional iSTAR workflow uses GPL-3.0 software; that component and its patch retain their upstream terms.

The local UNI extraction, CellViT patch preparation, and HD aggregation wrappers derive from this project's preprocessing code; their source paths and hashes are recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`. The public wrapper emits CellViT patches with row=y and col=x as required by the pinned upstream inference implementation. Global centroids/contours are xy; upstream bounding boxes are row/column. Newly generated ReSCOPE configs preserve the integer pixel stride used by extraction. Archived inputs/configurations are not rewritten, so rerunning the new raw-image pipeline is not a claim of bitwise reproduction of archived experiment coordinates.
